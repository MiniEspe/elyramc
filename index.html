FAQ

Before you start

Is ElyraMC really free?

Yes. No licence, no account, no subscription. Download the jar and play.

What do I need to run it?

Minecraft 26.1.2, 26.2 or 26.3 with Fabric Loader and Fabric API installed. ElyraMC is a single jar that goes in your mods folder — pick the one for your version on the Download page.

How do I open the menu?

Join a world or a server and press Right Shift. You can change the key in the menu's Settings.

Does it need a web panel or a Discord bot?

No. Everything — every macro, setting and notification — lives inside the game.

Is it safe to use?

Macros are against Hypixel's rules, and no mod can promise you will never be caught. ElyraMC is built to behave like a person and stops everything on a ban screen, but you use it at your own risk.
